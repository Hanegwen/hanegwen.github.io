Petri: Microbe Swarm: Privacy Policy

Nunchuck Designs · Last updated November 22, 2026

In short: Petri does not collect, share or sell your personal data. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress and a small play log stay on your device. To help balance the game, Petri can send anonymous play stats (such as battle results and crashes) to us; you can turn this off any time in Settings (see Anonymous play stats). The free version on iPhone shows non-personalized ads from Google AdMob between runs (see Ads); buying Remove Ads turns them off.

What stays on your device

  • Progress and settings: beaten levels, saved runs, achievements, Biomass, upgrades and your settings are saved on your device only.
  • A local play log: the game writes a small log of each play session (for example which level you played, how long a battle took, whether you won, the device model and OS version, and any errors). It helps fix bugs and tune difficulty. It contains no name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifier, and it uses a random ID created on your device instead of a device identifier. The log is kept inside the app (at most about 20 MB; older files are deleted automatically) and is never uploaded as a whole. It only leaves your device if you choose to send it to us with a bug report. The anonymous play stats below are a small summary taken from it.

What we collect

We collect no personal data. The only thing the app sends to us is the optional anonymous play stats summary. We do not use third-party analytics services and do not track you across other apps or websites. The only third party is Google AdMob, which serves ads in the free mobile version as described under Ads. The Steam (PC) version has no ads.

Anonymous play stats

To see how the game plays for real players and to balance it, Petri can send us a small summary of how you play. It is optional:

  • On Steam (PC and Steam Deck) the game asks once, on first launch (or the first launch after the update). Nothing is sent unless you choose “Share stats”.
  • On iPhone and iPad it is on by default.
  • Everywhere you can turn it on or off any time in Settings → “Share anonymous play stats”. Turning it off stops sending at once and deletes anything not yet sent from your device.

What is sent: results of battles and runs (level, win or loss, length, choices like the rewards you picked), how far you got in the tutorial, whether an ad was shown or skipped and whether Remove Ads was bought, the type of any crash or error and where in the game's code it happened, frame-rate summaries, and the game's build number, platform, device type (for example “iPhone” or “Steam Deck”), operating system version (for example “iOS 26”) and language setting. Each upload carries a random ID the game created on your device, so we can tell play sessions apart.

What is never sent: your name, email, account or Steam ID, contacts, location, the device's advertising or vendor identifiers (IDFA / IDFV), the device model or name, or the full play log. We do not store your IP address or other request details.

How it is used: only by us, to find bugs and tune difficulty. It is not used for advertising or tracking, not linked to you, and never sold or shared. The stats are stored on Cloudflare (our hosting provider) and deleted after 60 days; we may keep counts and averages made from them (for example a level's win rate) longer.

Test and development builds of the game never send play stats.

Children

Petri is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data, including progress and the play log, from your device. Play stats already sent carry only the random ID, so we cannot tell which ones are yours; they are deleted automatically after 60 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: mmorrone@nunchuckdesigns.com

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version may show ads between runs, never during a battle. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google's ad software on your device sends Google information such as an approximate location based on your IP address, an app-specific device identifier, ad interactions, and crash and performance data, so it can show the ad, count it and prevent fraud.

  • Ads are non-personalized: they are not based on your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • The app never asks to track you and never reads your advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not receive or store this information ourselves, and we never sell data.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first.
  • Buying Remove Ads turns ads off, and Google's ad software then stops making requests. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
